About us

A home away from home for every yatri

Atithi Bhavan is run with one simple idea: every guest should feel looked after. Our rooms are cleaned daily, linen is changed for every new guest, and the kitchen serves fresh, satvik vegetarian meals with Jain options on request.

We are a short walk from the main market and well connected to the railway station, bus stand and nearby temples, so families, senior citizens and large groups can move around easily.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a room?

Fill in the booking form above and tap “Send request”, or call or WhatsApp the front desk. We confirm availability and the final tariff before you travel.

Do you serve Jain food?

Yes. Our bhojanshala serves fresh vegetarian meals, and Jain food without onion, garlic or root vegetables is available on request.

Is advance payment required?

For festival dates and group bookings we take a small advance by UPI or bank transfer. For regular dates you can pay at check-in.

Can large groups and yatra sanghs stay together?

Yes. We have dormitory halls and family rooms on the same floor, and we can arrange meals for the whole group. Please message us with your dates and group size.

Is parking available?

Limited parking is available for cars and two-wheelers. Bus parking can be arranged nearby with advance notice.
